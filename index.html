About Me

I'm a forward-deployed style Applied AI & Full-Stack Engineer. I take ambiguous, high-stakes problems from the first stakeholder conversation through to global production — equally comfortable in a customer workshop, a security review, and a terminal. My work lives at the intersection of agentic systems, vision-language models, and generative 3D, and I measure success by systems that actually ship and include everyone.

At Visa I invented the first PIN on Glass entry method globally that met all industry security requirements to enable users with disabilities to securely enter their own confidential information — a problem security stakeholders called impossible — and drove it through security review, user research, and worldwide deployment (patent awarded to the team; live across Square, PayPal, and major processors). I also solo-built A11y SnapCheck, a full-stack vision-language platform that intercepts Figma webhooks, runs multi-LLM orchestration for accessibility analysis, and generates actionable reports across Visa Global Design — plus the enterprise agent benchmarking & regression-gating system, a 12-tool self-healing MCP server embedded in Visa's agentic toolchain, and production React & Flutter component libraries for BLITZ and the open-source Visa Product Design System (VPDS).

Outside work I build across the generative and agentic frontier. ForgeDNA and HermesForge pair a schema-driven text-to-image-to-3D pipeline (ComfyUI diffusion, TRELLIS.2 image-to-3D, SkinTokens rigging, Puppeteer animation) with a custom Godot 4.7 editor distribution and 21-tool MCP bridge. Daggr Studio turns natural-language intent into self-healing Gradio 6 workflows backed by a live-probed Hugging Face Space registry. I run Hermes Agent daily in production across a distributed Tailscale/tmux/RTX GPU mesh and contribute open-source skills, CLI toolkits, and core features upstream to Nous Research.

I'm also a 0-to-1 founder: I bootstrapped Kynda Coffee profitably from nothing while working three jobs through self-paid college — building and self-hosting its entire technology stack and Beancount accounting ledger myself — then shipped open-source platforms including SynapseJourney.org (polymath learning & open-science hub with pgvector RAG, 3D knowledge maps, BYOC compute routing, and native MCP), MenuMetrics.org (gram-level recipe costing & agentic hospitality ops), and AllergoScope (citation-verified immunology & agrochemical research workbench).
